## Consistency notes

The single most heavily weighted criterion is that the character stays the same
person across shots. How that was held here:

- Avatar locked to a fixed reference sheet before any clip was generated; every
  clip was checked against that sheet rather than against the previous clip, so
  drift could not accumulate.
- One room, one camera position and one wardrobe for all A-roll.
- Seeds recorded for every approved generation, so nothing needed re-rolling.
- Inserts kept below the nose line — no second face enters the video.

## Originality

The brand, product name, packaging and presenter are all invented. No element of
the reference ad's identity appears: not its name, logo, packaging, or the
likeness of the person in it. What carries over is structure only — hook
mechanics, cutting rhythm, and the order in which the argument is made.
